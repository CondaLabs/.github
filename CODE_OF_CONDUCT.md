# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

CondaLabs is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in our projects and community.

We welcome contributions and participation regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, nationality, appearance, or personal beliefs.

## Expected Behaviour

All community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Treat others with patience and empathy.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Respect differing opinions and engage in good-faith discussions.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language and conduct.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all CondaLabs project spaces and community interactions, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other channels used for project-related communication.

It also applies when someone is representing CondaLabs or its projects in public spaces.

Individual projects may provide additional community guidelines.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the CondaLabs organization maintainers through GitHub.

Please include enough information to help us understand and review the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

CondaLabs maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of the behaviour, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

- A private warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Removal from a project or community space.

Maintainers will aim to respond fairly and proportionately to reported incidents.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in CondaLabs projects and community spaces, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

We appreciate everyone who helps make CondaLabs a respectful and welcoming community.
